Optical Dipole Trap Calculator

Build a lab-frame ODT from Gaussian beams, visualize the geometry, and estimate trap depth, x/y/z curvatures, principal-mode frequencies, and photon scattering. Frequencies are computed from the smooth Gaussian envelope around the trap center.
